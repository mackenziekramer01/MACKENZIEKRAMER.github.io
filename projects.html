01 / RESEARCH · SPATIAL ANALYSIS

Rural–urban disparities in Alzheimer’s disease and related dementias

Question: How do geographic and social conditions relate to patterns in Alzheimer’s disease and related dementias across U.S. counties?

Approach: Scoping review and county-level analysis incorporating social vulnerability and built-environment measures, with machine-learning methods to explore patterns and model interpretation.

What to show: Research question, data sources, geographic methodology, key visualizations, limitations, and publication link. Be explicit that observed relationships are not evidence of causation.

02 / HEALTHCARE · ANALYTICS

Healthcare partnership performance and decision support

Question: How can recurring operational and utilization data be made easier for stakeholders to interpret and act on?

Approach: Reporting automation, KPI scorecards, dashboard development, and executive-facing communication.

What to show: A synthetic or fully de-identified example dashboard, workflow diagram, before-and-after process, and explanation of the decisions the tool supports.

03 / PERSONAL DATA SCIENCE · PREVENTATIVE HEALTH

Wearable data, daily habits, and wellbeing

Question: Can combining wearable metrics with self-reported context help reveal patterns related to recovery, mood, and daytime focus?

Approach: A personal research tool combining wearable data with structured self-surveys, exploratory analysis, and carefully evaluated recommendations.

What to show: Data model, sample dashboard, feature definitions, lagged analysis, and safeguards against overstating correlation or prediction.
