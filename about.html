I’m interested in the space between understanding a problem and doing something useful about it.

My background

I have an academic background in population health and biomedical data science and informatics, and I’m currently pursuing an MBA. My work has brought together healthcare analytics, research, data visualization, and cross-functional collaboration.

I’m especially interested in work that makes complex information more accessible and helps people make thoughtful decisions.

What I’m interested in

Healthcare innovation, population health, human-centered analytics, applied AI, preventative health, and the ways data can help organizations understand what is happening and what might be possible next.

Outside the spreadsheet

I’m a former collegiate basketball player, a lifelong learner, and someone who enjoys writing about growth, perspective, and the gray areas that make life interesting.
